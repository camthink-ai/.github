# Think Beyond Vision

👋 We're CamThink. We build Edge AI hardware you can program for your own vision projects.

Want to change when a camera wakes up, try a different model, or connect its output to your app? That's what these repos are for. Alongside the code, we share hardware resources and docs to help you work with the boards themselves.

## 🤝 Build with Us

Tell us how it runs on your hardware. Your camera setup, lighting, and power supply may be different from ours, and those details help us work through problems together.

- Open an issue with your board or device model, firmware version, logs, and steps to reproduce the problem.
- Send a pull request for a fix or an example. Include how you tested it.
- Try a proposed change on your device and post the results on the pull request.
- Fix a confusing step in the [docs](https://github.com/camthink-ai/wiki-documents), or help another developer get past it.

You can also drop a photo or demo into [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/6TZb2Y8WKx). Work in progress is welcome, including the bits you're still figuring out. 🔧

## From Prototype to Production

Need to fit a CamThink board into your product? Start by testing a complete device, then talk to us about the camera, connectivity, firmware, or enclosure changes you need. We support board-level integration and OEM/ODM development.
